3. [英NscaleがIPOへ、AI集中投資に市場は熱を保てるか](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/22/nscales-ipo-will-test-wall-streets-appetite-for-concentrated-ai-bets-once-again/) (TechCrunch・英語)<br>

4. [アンソロピックとOpenAI、同日に低価格AIを発表](https://www.nikkei.com/article/DGXZQOGN2300D0T20C26A9000000/) (日経・会員限定)<br>
